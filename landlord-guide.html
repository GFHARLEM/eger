Landlord Guide

Best practices for trusted listings in Njoro.

1. List Honestly

Use real photos, accurate rent, and true amenity details. Include any restrictions (curfew, deposit rules, utility terms) upfront.

2. Keep Records

Maintain clear rental records, payment acknowledgments, and occupancy logs. Written confirmations help reduce disputes.

3. Fair Marketing

Avoid exaggerated claims and deceptive pricing. Transparent listing information builds trust and aligns with Kenyan consumer fairness principles.

4. Property Safety Basics

Ensure working locks, safe electrical fittings, clean water access where possible, and reasonable sanitation standards before listing.

5. Handling Bookings

For short-stay/Airbnb units, set clear check-in times, minimum stay, occupancy limits, and cancellation terms.

6. Communication Standards

Respond promptly, provide viewing instructions clearly, and avoid pressure tactics. Respectful communication reduces report risk.

7. Compliance Reminder

Landlords are responsible for complying with relevant Kenyan property, tax, and housing obligations applicable to their premises and rental model.

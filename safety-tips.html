Safety Tips

Practical safety checks for students and short-stay guests.

Before Viewing

Share your location with a friend, schedule daytime viewings, and avoid carrying large cash.

At the Property

Verify doors/windows lock properly, check water availability, inspect bathroom hygiene, and test power sockets where possible.

Price and Payment

Confirm total cost, deposit terms, utility charges, and refund conditions before paying. Request a written acknowledgment for every payment.

Identity and Authority

Confirm you are dealing with the landlord or authorized manager. Be cautious with urgent payment pressure and contradictory details.

For Airbnb/Short Stays

Confirm check-in process, host contact, occupancy limit, emergency exit route, and neighborhood access at night.

When to Report

Report misleading pricing, fake photos, unsafe conditions, or suspicious behavior through the platform report option.

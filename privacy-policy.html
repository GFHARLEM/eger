Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

NjoroNest collects and processes personal data to match tenants and landlords, improve listing safety, and support lawful property marketing in Kenya. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect names, phone numbers, listing details, booking details, reviews, report submissions, and technical usage data (such as browser type and timestamps).

2. How We Use Data

We use personal data to publish listings, process bookings, verify reports, communicate with users, prevent fraud, and maintain records for lawful property management operations.

3. Kenyan Legal Context

Our data handling approach is aligned with the principles of Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, including lawful processing, purpose limitation, data minimization, and appropriate security safeguards. We also consider Kenyan consumer protection expectations for transparency in pricing and listing information.

4. Property Marketing and Accuracy

Landlords must provide accurate listing information. False or misleading advertising is prohibited. We may remove or restrict listings that appear deceptive, unsafe, or unlawful.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers or competent authorities when required by law, legal process, fraud investigations, or safety concerns.

6. Retention

We keep records only for as long as needed for bookings, disputes, platform integrity, compliance, and security. We may retain certain records where required by law.

7. User Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and operational limits. Contact support through the published platform contact channels.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No system is fully risk-free, and users should also protect account and device access.

9. Legal Note

This policy is for platform transparency and is not legal advice. For formal legal interpretation, consult a qualified Kenyan advocate.
